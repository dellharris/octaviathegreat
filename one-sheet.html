Where you are

Threads is working. Your content plan is set, the bars are landing, and TikTok is backing it up. We do not touch that. It is the spark.

The gap

Instagram and Facebook are not giving you much natural engagement yet. That is normal when an audience has not been shown a reason to care there. Those platforms reward content made for them, plus people who already know you. Right now your bars are not native there, and your Threads fans do not know where else to find you.

What success looks like

Followers on Instagram and Facebook who came from your bars, engaging without being asked, and a growing email list heading into the album. Real targets get set together on the call.

Four moves, high level

  • 1Carry the proof. Take the bars that already win and remake them natively for Instagram and Facebook, using the fact that they work as the hook.
  • 2Give a reason to follow. Send your Threads and TikTok fans somewhere with something only there: full verses, alt takes, behind the scenes, early EP listens, Storm Bars.
  • 3Borrow audiences. Collab posts, remixes and features put your bars in front of other communities. The remix and collab post we started is the template.
  • 4Own the audience. Every platform points to your email list, downloads and album fund, so no algorithm can take it away.

What we need from you

  • Your best performing bars and clips from Threads and TikTok
  • A look at your Instagram and Facebook insights
  • Your Threads plan, so we fit around it

The full plan

This page is the direction, not the finished plan. We build the full plan with you on our call: cadence, formats, who does what, and the targets.
